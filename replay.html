Act IIThe question nobody asked

They asked which machine loses the most minutes. The better question is which station the line is waiting on. It was never the press. Since 28 September it is one leak tester, re-testing almost half of seal lot SL-27.

Act IIITwenty shifts

Who gets blamed, who is really slow.

Each dot is one station in one shift. Scroll, or use the tabs, to see the same twenty shifts three ways. Click any column to replay that shift.

01 · Blamed

What the dashboard ranks

PulseBoard counts the minutes a station spends BLOCKED or on HOLD. The press tops it in all 20 shifts, even when the line beats its target.

02 · Real

What the line waited on

Count instead the minutes each station was the one not waiting. Fastening paces the first week. From 28 September the tester takes over. The press never appears.

03 · Cause

Why week two changed

Seal lot SL-27 needs a second test on 45% of modules at their first attempt (1,066 of 2,390 modules). Counted per tester slot, those retests are 31% of all SL-27 attempts (1,066 of 3,456). Every retest takes a slot on the only tester, and output falls by roughly the retest count.

Exercise 2 asks

Why a replay, not an email?

  1. 01Direction of waiting

    Blocking spreads upstream from the constraint and starving spreads downstream. You can see the press is a victim, not a cause.

  2. 02Disagreement, live

    The dashboard meter climbs for ST10 while the tester never stops. An email can state that; the replay shows it minute by minute.

  3. 03When to act

    On 30 Sep the bottleneck flips from the tester to fastening at about 10:15. A shift-level verdict hides that the right action changed.

  4. 04Independent clocks agree

    The label hold ends at the 17:02 physical handover, not the 17:41 system posting. Only a time-aligned view settles the dispute.

Method and data limits
  • PLC states use controller time; received_at is 0–3 s later. About 1,600 events arrive out of order, so arrival order is never used. 78 resent rows are dropped.
  • Buffers are minute snapshots, held until the next one and never interpolated. Their station columns copy the PLC events exactly, so they are not independent evidence.
  • "Line waits on" is the station with the lowest BLOCKED + STARVED share over the trailing 15 min; within 2 points is a tie. HOLD and SETUP count as not waiting. In the first 15 min of 21 Sep the line fills from empty, so the press wins by construction.
  • Leak tests and scans have 1 s stamps but are binned per minute. A retest is a test slot, not a product. 83 serials miss a scan; 25 of them are scraps.
  • Shifts: day 08:00–13:59, evening 14:00–19:59. Nights and weekends are gaps and are not drawn.
  • The tester loop in Act V is an illustrative model using the observed attempt rate and RECHECK rates, not a replay of individual units.
  • Not in the extract: trip or alarm log, tester traces or RECHECK reasons, torque-tool data, per-station cycle times, field returns.
